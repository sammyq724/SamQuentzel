Welcome

I help old-school businesses work smarter with AI.

I'm Sam Quentzel, a sophomore at Olin Business School at Washington University in St. Louis, majoring in finance and entrepreneurship.

This past summer, at my father's wholesale plumbing supply company in Brooklyn, I built an AI system that reads a customer's request, slang and all, and matches every line to the right item in a catalog of more than 20,000 materials. It gets the item right 90% of the time, and the email pipeline around it saves the sales team about 20 hours a week.

On campus, I'm a teaching assistant for Management 1000 and Vice President of Events for the WashU Real Estate Club. Away from work, I lift, hike, train jiu-jitsu, and read philosophy.
