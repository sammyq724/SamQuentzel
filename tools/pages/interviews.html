Interview with my mother

A conversation with my mother, Sandy Quentzel, about her path from accounting to serving as mayor of Roslyn Harbor.

What I learned

My mother's path into accounting began by chance at a summer job, guided by a mentor she still remembers by name. Years later, she became mayor of Roslyn Harbor by answering an email for an unpaid volunteer role. Hearing that made me realize I don't need my whole future figured out right now. What matters more is building real skills and relationships and being open when unexpected opportunities appear.

Her COVID story also made me rethink what leadership means. It isn't about a title. It's about stepping up when people depend on you. I've watched her lead my whole life, but I never fully saw how her experiences shaped her. As I pursue a future in entrepreneurship and business leadership, I want to follow her advice: build genuine connections, treat every interaction like it matters, and work hard enough that I'm ready when my own unexpected door opens.
