Purpose Essay

The problem I want to spend my career on

It’s easy to say I want to cure cancer or end world hunger, but the problem that truly aligns with my goals is making the U.S. economy stronger, wealthier, and, most importantly, more efficient. As an AI enthusiast, I am passionate about helping firms and the broader economy operate at their full potential.

Inefficiency within firms persists for two reasons. First, markets tolerate it: Outside of extremely price-competitive industries like raw materials, firms can remain profitable without actually advancing their businesses. Second, because inefficiency carries little immediate cost, people resist change and don’t see it as important. I saw that resistance firsthand at my father’s plumbing supply company. I want to spend my career confronting this problem.

My business education has given me the foundation to do so, most notably through communication. Efficient organizations depend on clear communication, and through team projects and group work from WashU Olin classes, I have learned to convey and communicate high-level ideas and strategies. Adopting new tools like AI requires more than technical knowledge; it requires persuading people that change is worthwhile. By pairing my business training with that ability to communicate, I hope to help firms modernize and, in turn, strengthen the economy as a whole.
